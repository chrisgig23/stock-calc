How we handle your data

Privacy & Security

Your portfolio data is yours. We store what we need to run the app, protect it with standard security practices, and don't go looking at it unless something's broken. Here's the full picture.

The short version: We don't sell your data, share it with other users, or access it on a regular basis. Passwords are hashed, brokerage tokens are encrypted, and your holdings are only visible to you through the app. Like any web service, our team can technically reach the database — but we only do that when there's a real reason to, like fixing a bug or resolving a support issue.

What we store

We store what's needed to run your portfolio tracker and nothing more.

  • Your account names, holdings, transactions, snapshots, and allocation targets
  • Your username, hashed password, and verified email address
  • Brokerage connection tokens if you link an external account — stored encrypted

How we protect it

We follow standard security practices for web applications handling financial data.

  • Passwords are hashed — we can't read them, even if we wanted to
  • Schwab OAuth tokens are encrypted at the database level
  • Your data is scoped to your account — other users can't see it through the app
  • All traffic runs over HTTPS

What we do with your data

Your data powers your portfolio views, reports, and growth charts. That's it. We don't run ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't share it with third parties.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or monetize your portfolio data
  • We don't execute trades or hold your assets — WealthTrack is a tracker, not a brokerage
  • We don't use your data to train AI models or build advertising profiles

Can other users see what I own?

No. Your portfolio is only visible to your account. There's no social feed, no leaderboards, no sharing — your holdings stay private.

Can the WealthTrack team see my portfolio?

Technically yes, in the same way your bank's engineers can access your account when they need to. We have access to the database for maintenance, support, and debugging — but we don't browse user portfolios, and that access is only used when there's a specific reason.

Are my passwords or brokerage credentials stored in plaintext?

No. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes — we can't recover or read them. Schwab OAuth tokens are encrypted in the database.

Is WealthTrack end-to-end encrypted?

Not in the cryptographic sense — we're a standard web application, not a zero-knowledge vault. For most users, our approach (hashed credentials, encrypted tokens, scoped access, and HTTPS) is a solid baseline for a portfolio tracker. If that distinction matters to you, it's worth knowing upfront.
