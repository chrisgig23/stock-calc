Privacy & Security

WealthTrack stores your portfolio data to power your accounts and reports. We don't sell it, share it, or use it for anything else.

Passwords are hashed

We never store your password in plain text. It's converted to a one-way hash on the way in — we can't read it, and neither can anyone else.

Your data stays yours

Your portfolio is private to your account. Other users can't see your holdings, and we don't use your data for advertising or share it with third parties.

Brokerage tokens encrypted

If you connect a brokerage account, your OAuth tokens are encrypted before they're stored. Your login credentials never touch our servers.

Encrypted in transit

All communication between your browser and WealthTrack is protected by HTTPS. Your data is never sent over an unencrypted connection.
